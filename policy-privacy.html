📋 Information We Collect

When you shop with Misk, we collect only what's needed to serve you:

  • Order details: Name, phone number, delivery address, city, order notes
  • Payment info: Transaction ID and payment screenshot (for advance payments only)
  • Cart data: Items in your cart, stored on your own device (not sent to us)
  • Contact: Email or WhatsApp number if you reach out to us
  • Technical: Basic browser info for site functionality (no personal tracking)

We do not collect or store full card numbers, bank passwords, or OTPs.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To confirm and verify advance payment transactions
  • To communicate about your order (confirmation, dispatch, delivery)
  • To handle exchanges or issues you report
  • To improve our service and product selection
  • To send updates about new drops (only if you subscribe to our newsletter)

We will never sell your information to third parties.

🤝 When We Share Information

We only share your info in these specific cases:

  • Courier partners — to deliver your order (name, phone, address)
  • Payment verification — if you pay via advance, we check with the bank/wallet to confirm the transaction
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or government authority

All partners are required to protect your data and use it only for these purposes.

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage (localStorage) on your own device to:

  • Remember items in your cart
  • Keep you logged in where applicable
  • Remember your preferences (like filter selections)

This data stays on your device — you can clear it anytime via your browser settings. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies.

🛡️ Data Security

  • Orders are stored securely in Google Sheets (private, access-controlled)
  • Payment screenshots are stored in Google Drive with restricted access
  • Our website uses HTTPS encryption
  • Only authorized Misk staff can access order information
  • We retain order data for reasonable periods (typically 1–2 years for exchange/warranty purposes)

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% immune. If you ever suspect a breach, contact us immediately.

✋ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate info
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Unsubscribe from marketing messages anytime
  • Opt out of any future communication

To exercise any of these, contact us — we'll respond within 7 days.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.

Significant changes will be announced on this page and, where relevant, communicated via our WhatsApp or newsletter.
