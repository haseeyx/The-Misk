Returns & Exchanges

🕐 Last updated: November 2025

📋 Our Promise

Every Misk piece is quality-checked before dispatch. If something arrives damaged or incorrect, we'll make it right — as quickly and simply as possible.

📅
7-Day Exchange Window

Report any issue within 7 days of delivery to be eligible for an exchange.

🎥 Video Proof Required

🎬
Unboxing video is mandatory for damage claims

To protect both you and us, we require a short continuous video of you unboxing your order. This ensures the damage (if any) occurred before or during delivery — not after.

Your video must show:

  • The sealed package before you open it
  • Continuous footage while opening the parcel (no cuts or edits)
  • The damaged item clearly visible with any defects close-up
  • Original tags, packaging, and any inclusions

Claims without video will not be accepted for damage-related issues.

✅ What's Eligible

We accept exchanges for:

  • Damaged or defective items (with unboxing video)
  • Wrong item or size delivered
  • Missing pieces from a set
  • Significant colour or design mismatch from listing

Not eligible for exchange:

  • Sold Out or Final Sale items
  • Items without unboxing video (damage claims)
  • Used, washed, worn, or altered items
  • Damage caused by customer handling
  • Requests after 7 days from delivery
  • Change of mind (no defect)

💬 How to Request an Exchange

Follow these steps to submit your claim. Our team will respond within 24 hours.

1
Record your unboxing video

Continuous footage from sealed package to revealing the item — no edits.

2
Message us on WhatsApp

Send your Order ID, the video, and a brief description of the issue.

3
We review within 24 hours

Our team confirms eligibility and provides exchange instructions.

4
Ship the item back

We'll arrange or reimburse the return shipping for verified damage claims.
